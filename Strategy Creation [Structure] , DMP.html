Moving Average Strategy creation and Backtesting¶

STEP 1 : Coming up with a trading idea (Using 5 min data)¶

we determine the 12 period simple moving average and compare it with the price at that time .

There are two conditions which we check:

  1. If the Price is greater than SMA 12 , we go long . We continue to stay invested untill the square-off condition is satisfied.
  2. When the price becomes less than the SMA 12 , we square off our long postion

Our trading rules can be stated as:

  1. "BUY" when Price >SMA 12
  2. SQUARE off when price <= SMA12
